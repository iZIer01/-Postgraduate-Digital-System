# -Postgraduate-Digital-System

This system is a digital platform for NUST that helps manage all postgraduate activities in one place. It will make it easier for students, supervisors, and faculty staff to handle proposals, progress reports, and thesis submissions without using paper or emails.

The system will allow students to upload their progress reports, track how far they are with their studies, and see feedback from their supervisors and the Higher Degrees Committee.

Supervisors will use the system to review student work, give comments, approve progress reports, and submit proposals or final theses to the Head of Department. They will also be able to monitor their students’ progress.

Internal evaluators will check student proposals and approve them before they move to the next stage. External evaluators will mark final theses, update their profiles, and submit claims for payment through the system.

The Head of Department (HoD) will manage submissions, assign evaluators, and forward approved work to the next level. The FPGC-R will take these submissions to the Higher Degrees Committee for discussion and final decisions. The FPGC will handle student selection and assign external examiners.

Overall, the system will improve communication, reduce delays, keep records in one place, and make the whole postgraduate process faster and more organized.
